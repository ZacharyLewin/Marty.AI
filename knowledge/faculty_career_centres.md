# Faculty career centres

Besides CaPS, several faculties run their own career offices. A student can use CaPS **and** their faculty office.

## Desautels: Soutar Career Centre (Management)
- Desautels' Career Management team works out of the Soutar Career Centre, at 1001 Sherbrooke St. West, Room 650. Phone 514-398-2996 · careercentre.mgmt@mcgill.ca
- Serves BCom, MBA, MMA and MMF students with coaching, workshops, speaker series and employer events.
- Signature events:
  - **Jaclyn Fisher Career Fair** (September). Open to all faculties.
  - **Toronto Meet McGill** (late January). A recruiting and networking trip that exposes Desautels students to the Toronto job market. The 2026 edition ran 28–30 January.
- Recruiting runs early. Finance and consulting internship recruiting for the following summer starts in the fall, so U1/U2 students interested in banking or consulting should be preparing by September.
- Links: https://www.mcgill.ca/desautels/career · portal: management.myfuture.mcgill.ca

## Engineering Career Centre
- McGill Engineering Student Centre, Frank Dawson Adams Building, 3450 University St., Room 22 · 514-398-8100 · careers4engineers@mcgill.ca
- Runs **TechFair**, McGill's big engineering and tech fair (late September / early October at Centre Mont-Royal).
- https://www.mcgill.ca/careers4engineers

## Law: Career Development Office (CDO)
- 3644 Peel St., Room 416 · 514-398-6159
- Handles articling and summer recruitment, alumni mentorship, and job postings on the 12Twenty portal.
- https://www.mcgill.ca/cdo

## Education Career Services
- Runs the Education Career Fair (February) and Kinesiology Career Exploration Day. Contact career.education@mcgill.ca

## Which office should I use?
- **Arts, Science, everyone else:** CaPS is your home base.
- **Management, Engineering, Law, Education:** start with your faculty office for recruiting, and use CaPS for broader exploration, the Mentor Program and workshops.

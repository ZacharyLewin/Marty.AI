# Clubs and getting involved

## SSMU: the umbrella
The Students' Society of McGill University (SSMU) is the undergraduate student union. It runs 250+ clubs across about ten categories (arts, culture & religion, environment, games & hobbies, media, politics, academic & professional networks, and more). It also runs a set of student-run **Services** (peer support, safety, food and outreach services) and recognises larger **Independent Student Groups (ISGs)**, which are separate non-profits.

- Club directory: https://ssmu.ca/student-life/clubs-services-isg
- Most clubs are free to join. Some charge a small fee depending on what they do.
- The SSMU job board lists paid and volunteer positions.
- Student groups post events to the **SSMU Campus Calendar**.
- Grad students are welcome in SSMU clubs, and **PGSS** runs grad-specific committees.
- Macdonald Campus students: the **MCSS** (Macdonald Campus Students' Society) runs its own clubs and a Club Night.

## Activities Night
The single best way to find your people. It's SSMU's club fair, held once a semester in the SSMU building (the University Centre, 3480 McTavish, still called "Shatner" by most students). It runs in the first or second week of classes in **September and January**. Every club, service and group gets a table. You walk around, talk to the execs, and sign up for mailing lists. It's free, and you don't need to register as a student.
- Info: https://ssmu.ca/student-life/activities-night · activitiesnight@ssmu.ca

## Faculty societies
Every faculty has an undergraduate society that runs Frosh, faculty pubs, academic events and career events:
- **AUS**, Arts Undergraduate Society (co-runs Work Your BA and the Grad & Professional Schools Fair)
- **SUS**, Science Undergraduate Society
- **MUS**, Management Undergraduate Society
- **EUS**, Engineering Undergraduate Society
- Plus EdUS (Education), the Law Students' Association, and others. Departmental associations sit under these, for example a maths, philosophy or economics students' association.

## Career-focused clubs
### Finance and investing (mostly under the MUS, open to all faculties)
- **McGill Investment Club (MIC).** McGill's largest undergrad investment club, 700+ members. Runs an internal Investment Committee/analyst program and the Toronto & Montreal Finance Conventions, a Women in Finance Convention and a Global Stock Pitch Competition.
- **RedPoint Capital.** Alternative asset classes (PE, VC, hedge funds).
- **Martlet Fund.** A student-run investment group.
- **McGill Markets Review.** Financial markets and equity research.
- **Engineering Investment Group.** Finance for engineers (EUS).

### Consulting
- **McGill Consulting Association (MCA).** Recruitment-focused: case prep, mock interviews, office tours.
- **JED Consulting.** A student-run consulting firm with roughly 30 student consultants a semester, working on real client projects.
- **NORD Consulting.** Pro-bono consulting for non-profits.
- **Consulting Academy McGill.** Skills-building for students new to consulting.

### Tech, data and entrepreneurship
- **DTech** (business × technology), **McGill Data Network**, **Blockchain at McGill**, **McGill Entrepreneurs Society**, **McGill Ventures**
- The **McGill Dobson Centre for Entrepreneurship** runs startup programs and mentors.

### Other professional clubs under the MUS
Desautels Women in Business, International Management Association, McGill Marketing Network, McGill Sports Management Club, Real Estate Club, Fashion Business Uncovered, Entertainment Management Conference, McGill Business Review, Desautels Accounting Society, Desautels Sustainability Network.

Grad (Desautels): Desautels Graduate Consulting Club, Desautels Graduate Finance Club, McGill Business Consulting Group, McGill Case Competition Association.

## Insider advice
- Competitive clubs (MIC analyst roles, JED, MCA) often recruit in the **first two or three weeks** of each semester, sometimes with applications and interviews. Show up to Activities Night ready to ask "when does recruitment open?"
- You don't need to be in Desautels to join MUS clubs. Arts and Science students join them all the time.
- Pick one "career" club and one "joy" club. The second is often where the friendships come from.

# CaPS (Career Planning Service): what it does and how to use it

CaPS is McGill's central career office, open to students from every faculty. It offers career advising and counselling, workshops, career exploration programs, experiential learning, mentoring, and career and networking events.

## Where and how to reach it
- **Downtown:** Brown Student Services Building, 3600 McTavish, Suite 2200 (the "CaPS Design Lab" workshops are in room 2200)
- **Macdonald Campus:** Centennial Centre, 21111 Lakeshore Road, Ste-Anne-de-Bellevue
- **Phone:** 514-398-3304 · **Email:** careers.caps@mcgill.ca
- Website: https://www.mcgill.ca/caps/

## myFuture: the important caveat
myFuture (https://caps.myfuture.mcgill.ca) is McGill's career platform. It holds job and internship postings, workshop and info-session sign-ups, advising appointments and employer profiles. **CaPS events now live inside myFuture**, so the full events calendar is behind McGill login. Marty can't see it. If a student asks "what CaPS workshops are on this week?", point them to myFuture and share whatever public events Marty does know about.

The faculty career offices each run their own myFuture portal:
- Desautels (Soutar Career Centre): management.myfuture.mcgill.ca
- Engineering Career Centre: engineering.myfuture.mcgill.ca
- Law Career Development Office: law.myfuture.mcgill.ca (Law also uses 12Twenty)

## Services
- **One-on-one advising.** Book through myFuture. Covers career direction, CV and cover-letter review, interview prep and job-search strategy.
- **Drop-in advising.** Short same-day slots, in person or online, first come first served. Good for a quick CV check. Schedule: https://mcgill.ca/caps/students/services/advising
- **Workshops.** CVs, LinkedIn, interviews, networking, French in the workplace, and job-search sprints for graduating students.
- **McGill Mentor Program** (CaPS + McGill Alumni Association). Matches students with McGill alumni for career advice. It isn't a job-placement service. One mentor at a time, and you can apply for a new one once a match ends. Grad students are eligible. Contact: mentor.caps@mcgill.ca · https://www.mcgill.ca/caps/students/services/mentor
- **Job databases and the resource centre.** Industry guides, graduate-school info, international opportunities.
- **Career fairs.** See the career fairs file.

## Seasonal programming
- **Work Your BA** (CaPS + Arts Undergraduate Society) runs every January to February. Panels with Arts alumni, career chats and a "Job Search Extravaganza". Made for Arts students who aren't sure where a BA leads. Registration is through myFuture.

## Eligibility
Current students get everything. Recent alumni keep most services, but info sessions and on-campus recruiting are reserved for current graduating students, and alumni can't join the mentoring and job-shadowing programs.

# Students | McGill University

## Student Services at McGill

McGill provides a comprehensive suite of student services to support academic success,
health, housing, career development, and community life.

## Key Offices

### Registrar's Office
Handles course registration, transcripts, degree verification, and academic standing.
- Website: mcgill.ca/registrar
- Phone: 514-398-7878

### Financial Aid & Awards Office (FAAO)
Bursaries, scholarships, emergency funding, and work-study.
- Website: mcgill.ca/studentaid
- Phone: 514-398-6013

### Student Wellness Hub
Counselling, psychiatry, and health services.
- Website: mcgill.ca/wellness
- Phone: 514-398-6017

### Career Planning Service (CaPS)
Career advising, resume review, job postings, and recruiting events.
- Website: mcgill.ca/caps
- Phone: 514-398-3304

### International Student Services (ISS)
Immigration advice, cultural programs, and international student support.
- Website: mcgill.ca/internationalstudents
- Phone: 514-398-4349

### Residences & Student Housing
On-campus housing applications, off-campus housing registry.
- Website: mcgill.ca/student-housing
- Phone: 514-398-6368

## Student Portal — Minerva

Minerva (minerva.mcgill.ca) is McGill's online student portal.
Use it to:
- Register for courses
- View your unofficial transcript
- Pay tuition and fees
- Apply for bursaries
- Check your financial aid status
- Accept or defer admissions offers

## myCourses

McGill uses myCourses (based on Moodle) for course management:
- Access lecture materials, syllabi, and assignments
- Submit work and receive grades
- Communicate with instructors
- URL: mycourses.mcgill.ca

## Student Life

McGill has over 300 student clubs and associations. The Students' Society of McGill
University (SSMU) is the main undergraduate student association.
- SSMU: ssmu.ca
- Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies: mcgill.ca/gps

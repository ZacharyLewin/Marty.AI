# Student Wellness | McGill University

## Student Wellness Hub

The Student Wellness Hub brings together mental health, physical health, and accessibility services
under one roof to support McGill students holistically.

Location: Brown Student Services Building, 3600 McTavish Street
Hours: Monday–Friday, 9 AM – 5 PM (extended hours available during exams)

## Counselling Services

Free, confidential counselling is available to all registered McGill students.

### How to Access Counselling

1. Call 514-398-6017 or visit the Wellness Hub in person to make an appointment.
2. For urgent mental health concerns, same-day urgent appointments are available.
3. Online appointments (Zoom) are available — ask when booking.

### Services Available

- Individual counselling (short-term, solution-focused)
- Group therapy programs (anxiety, relationships, grief, academic stress)
- Psychiatric consultations (for medication assessment)
- Crisis intervention

## Peer Support — Nightline

Nightline is a student-run, peer-support phone line:

- **Hours:** Sunday–Friday, 6 PM – 3 AM
- **Phone:** 514-398-6246
- **Confidential and non-judgmental**

## Psychiatry & Medication

Students requiring psychiatric assessment can be referred by a counsellor or their family doctor.
The Wellness Hub has an on-site psychiatrist for consultation.

## International Student Wellness

International students face unique stressors (language, culture, isolation).
Dedicated international student support is available — ask specifically when booking.

## Wellness Self-Help Resources

- **MindWell-U:** Free online mindfulness courses at mindwell-u.com
- **Headspace:** Free subscription for McGill students (log in with your McGill email)
- **Therapy Assistance Online (TAO):** mcgill.ca/wellness/online-resources

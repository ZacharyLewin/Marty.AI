# Financial Aid & Bursaries | McGill University

## Overview

McGill offers a range of financial aid options for students in need, including bursaries,
emergency funding, work-study positions, and external scholarship referrals.
The Financial Aid & Awards Office (FAAO) administers most of these programs.

## Bursaries

Bursaries are non-repayable grants awarded based on financial need. McGill's bursary fund
is one of the largest in Canada.

### Fall/Winter Bursary Application

- **Application opens:** September 15
- **Deadline:** October 31 (for priority consideration)
- **Late applications:** Accepted until January 31 (reduced funding pool)

To apply: log in to Minerva → Student Menu → Financial Aid → Bursary Application.

### Eligibility

- Enrolled as a full-time or part-time McGill student
- Demonstrated financial need (assessed via your financial information submitted in Minerva)
- Canadian citizen, permanent resident, or protected person (international students: see note below)

### International Students

International students are eligible for a separate international bursary fund.
Apply through the same Minerva portal. Note: international bursaries are more limited.

## Emergency Financial Assistance

Students facing sudden financial hardship (unexpected medical costs, family emergency, etc.)
can apply for emergency bursary funding year-round.

Contact: studentaid.mcgill.ca or visit the FAAO office at 3600 McTavish Street, Suite 3200.

## Work-Study Program

McGill's Work-Study program offers on-campus employment to financially eligible students.
Positions are posted on McGill's Careers website. Hours are limited to protect academic progress.

## OSAP / Quebec AFE

- **Quebec residents:** Apply for Aide financière aux études (AFE) at afe.gouv.qc.ca
- **Ontario residents:** Apply for OSAP at ontario.ca/osap
- McGill's Student Accounts office can certify your enrollment for loan purposes.

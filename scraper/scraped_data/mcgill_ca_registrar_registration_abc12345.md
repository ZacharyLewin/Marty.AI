# Registration | McGill University

## Course Registration

McGill's course registration system is called Minerva (the student portal, separate from this advisor).
Students register for courses through Minerva at minerva.mcgill.ca.

## How to Register

1. Log in to Minerva at minerva.mcgill.ca with your McGill username and password.
2. Navigate to Student Menu → Registration → Add/Drop Courses.
3. Enter the course Reference Number (CRN) or search by subject/course number.
4. Review prerequisites before adding. The system will flag conflicts.
5. Save your changes — courses are not reserved until saved.

## Registration Holds

Your registration may be blocked by a hold. Common holds include:
- Outstanding tuition balance
- Missing immunization records
- Academic standing hold (GPA below requirement)

To clear a hold, visit the relevant office (Registrar, Student Accounts, or Student Wellness).

## Maximum Course Load

- Standard full-time: 15 credits per semester
- Maximum with permission: 18 credits per semester
- Part-time students: 11 credits or fewer

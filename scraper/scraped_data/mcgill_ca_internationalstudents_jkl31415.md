# International Students | McGill University

## International Student Services (ISS)

McGill's International Student Services office supports students from over 150 countries.
ISS assists with study permits, immigration questions, cultural adjustment, and social integration.

Location: 3415 McTavish Street (Thomson House)
Email: iss.info@mcgill.ca | Phone: 514-398-4349

## Study Permits

All international students (except Americans and some exchange students) need a valid study permit
to study in Canada.

### Before You Arrive

- Apply for your study permit as early as possible — processing times can exceed 12 weeks.
- You will need your McGill Letter of Acceptance (sent after paying your deposit).
- Apply at ircc.canada.ca (online recommended).

### Extending Your Study Permit

Apply at least 30 days before your permit expires. You may continue studying while your renewal
is in process ("implied status").

## Social Insurance Number (SIN)

International students with a study permit that includes the condition "may work" are eligible
to apply for a Social Insurance Number at a Service Canada office.

A SIN is required to work on or off campus.

## Working in Canada

- **On campus:** Unlimited hours — no work permit required.
- **Off campus:** Up to 20 hours/week during the semester; full-time during scheduled breaks.
  (As of 2024, Canada allows 24 hours/week off campus — verify current rules with ISS.)
- **Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP):** Eligible graduates can apply for a PGWP of up to 3 years.

## Health Insurance

International students are automatically enrolled in the McGill International Health Insurance Plan
(Blue Cross), billed through your Student Accounts. If you have comparable coverage, you may opt out
within the first few weeks of each semester.

## Arrival Checklist

1. Confirm study permit is valid and matches your intended program/duration.
2. Register with ISS upon arrival (required within 30 days of starting your studies).
3. Set up your McGill email and Minerva account.
4. Attend the International Student Orientation.
5. Apply for a SIN if you plan to work.
